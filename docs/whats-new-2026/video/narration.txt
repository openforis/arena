Welcome to Open Foris Arena. Here is what is new in twenty twenty six: a year of new features, from survey design to data analysis.
It was a year of steady releases: seventy one versions of Arena, eighteen headline features, and three new interface languages: Portuguese, Japanese and Serbian.
Arena has a fresh look. The new dashboard shows records, contributors and a map of your data at a glance. And you can now switch between a light and a dark theme.
Survey designers get smarter forms. An attribute can be required only when a condition is true. An expression can decide which rows an enumerated entity gets. And any attribute can be cloned, with all its properties, into another entity.
Preview your survey as it looks in Arena Mobile: on a phone or a tablet, in portrait or landscape, right in the browser, with no device needed.
Moving surveys between servers is easier. Compare a survey with one from another server, and apply only the changes you pick. You can also create a survey from an O D K form, and import its data.
With branding, each survey gets its own look: up to three logos, a background image, and a primary colour applied to the whole application.
Records can now be printed, in Word or P D F, following the form layout, with pictures, a Q R code, and your own letterhead.
Data entry keeps your data cleaner. When a change makes some fields no longer relevant, their values are cleared, after asking you to confirm.
Security is stronger, with two factor authentication, Q R code login for Arena Mobile, and finer control over which user groups can see which records.
In the Data Explorer, export species by records tables for vegetation analysis tools. And, new in version two point twelve, show totals and percentages in aggregate mode.
The map has a brand new workspace. Add your own layers, from K M L, Shapefile and Geo Jason files, to W M S services. Measure distances and areas, take screenshots, and search by coordinates or record keys.
And now, the highlight of version two point twelve: areas of interest. In the survey info, add one or more polygons, for example the municipalities of a pilot area, and check them on a map while you add them. They also appear as layers in the data map.
Areas of interest work in expressions. Geo distance to A O I boundary gives the distance from a location to the edge of an area: negative inside, positive outside. Compare it with the G P S accuracy, and tell field teams right away if a plot is inside, outside, or uncertain. It works offline in Arena Mobile, too.
For analysis, run Whisp on every sampling point in the background, and build richer processing chains, with two phase sampling and templates.
Optional A I features help survey designers: write expressions from plain language, explain existing ones, and translate labels in one click.
Ask in plain language: the A I builds Data Explorer queries for you, and a help chatbot answers your questions about Arena. You choose the A I provider.
Under the hood, Arena is faster. Importing large records now takes seconds instead of minutes, records are lighter, and duplicates are merged automatically.
System administrators get everything on one page: live server logs, user backup and restore, messages, and more.
Thank you for watching! The full changelog and the source code are on GitHub, at github dot com slash openforis slash arena.
