Open Foris Arena: logo concepts

Proof of concepts based on the current mark (data streams flowing into a grid, blue ring, green core). Each card shows the mark at 150, 64, 32 and 16 px, on a dark background, and next to the wordmark.

1 · Evolved

Your current mark redrawn flat: four streams flow into a 4×4 grid that breaks out through the ring. The safest change.

2 · Monogram

A pixel "A" built from data cells and fed by streams, on a rounded app-icon tile. Strong as a favicon and mobile icon.

Open Foris Arena

3 · Leaf

A leaf: organic veins on one side, a data grid on the other. Links field inventories (forests) to digital data.

Open Foris Arena

4 · Rings → raster

Tree rings on the left; the same rings rasterized into cells on the right. "From nature to data" in one shape.

Open Foris Arena

5 · Pixel canopy

A tree whose crown is a data grid and whose trunk is three merging streams. A nod to the Open Foris tree.

Open Foris Arena

6 · Minimal flow

Three lines settle and become cells, blue to green. No ring, the most minimal and scalable option.
